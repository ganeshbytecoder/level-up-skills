## Similar to Hindi *Lokokti* (लोकोक्ति)

English **proverbs** are very similar to Hindi **लोकोक्तियाँ (Lokoktiyan)**. They express general truths or life lessons.

Examples:

| English Proverb                              | Hindi Equivalent                      |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| A stitch in time saves nine.                 | समय पर किया काम बड़ा नुकसान बचाता है। |
| As you sow, so shall you reap.               | जैसी करनी वैसी भरनी।                  |
| Empty vessels make the most noise.           | अधजल गगरी छलकत जाए।                   |
| All that glitters is not gold.               | हर चमकने वाली चीज़ सोना नहीं होती।    |
| United we stand, divided we fall.            | एकता में बल है।                       |
| Don't count your chickens before they hatch. | अंडे फूटने से पहले चूज़े मत गिनो।     |
| Where there's a will, there's a way.         | जहाँ चाह, वहाँ राह।                   |
| Every cloud has a silver lining.             | हर बुरे समय के बाद अच्छा समय आता है।  |
| Look before you leap.                        | सोच-समझकर कदम उठाओ।                   |
| Honesty is the best policy.                  | ईमानदारी सबसे अच्छी नीति है।          |

### For advanced English

To sound natural in conversation, you should also learn:

* **250+ idioms** (e.g., *spill the beans*, *hit the nail on the head*)
* **500+ collocations** (e.g., *make a decision*, *strong evidence*)
* **200+ phrasal verbs** (e.g., *carry on*, *figure out*)




* **300+ common fixed expressions** (e.g., *by the way*, *at the end of the day*)
* **200+ similes** (e.g., *as cool as a cucumber*)
* **100+ famous quotations** commonly referenced in English

Together, these will make your English sound much more fluent and idiomatic.
