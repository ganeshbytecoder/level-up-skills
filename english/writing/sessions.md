Situation :
Our team manages the Allocation Review Service (ARS), a critical platform that processes billions of inventory forecasting records used to validate purchase orders across Walmart's supply chain. As data volumes increased, the platform experienced rising cloud costs and scalability challenges.
challenge :
The primary challenge was reducing infrastructure costs without compromising performance, scalability, and system reliability.
Action :
