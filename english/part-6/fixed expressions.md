Learning **fixed expressions** is one of the fastest ways to sound like a native speaker. These are groups of words that native speakers use as a unit. Unlike idioms, most fixed expressions have a **literal meaning**, but they occur so often that fluent speakers recognize them instantly.

Below are **300+ of the most useful fixed expressions**, organized by function.

## How to master them

Instead of memorizing all 300+ at once, learn **10 expressions per week**. For each one:

1. Learn the meaning.
2. Read 3–5 example sentences.
3. Write 5 sentences of your own.
4. Use it in conversation several times that week.
5. Review it after 1 day, 1 week, and 1 month.

This approach helps the expressions become part of your active vocabulary rather than something you only recognize when reading.
